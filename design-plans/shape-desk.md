# Object shape — Desk

Matrix §15. The Desk is the product's home: it opens on the morning (what waits, what the agents did, who is on
shift), sets the decision to take first apart, and keeps « Your day » a §14 queue — but a queue of the agents' objects
(posts, conversations, metrics, clips), not of tickets.

## References (Mobbin)
- **Asana · Home** — greeting with the date, then widgets of my work. Kept: the greeting as the page's head, not a
  sentence about counts.
- **hilos · Home** — « Welcome back », four coloured stat tiles (Unread, On your plate, Shipped, In motion) and a
  « People and agents » block. Kept: the numbers as named tiles in one band, the agents present on the home page.
- **Linear · Inbox / My issues** — list with a detail pane, issues grouped by status under short headers with a count.
  Kept: master-detail and J/K, short headers by gesture with their count, nothing else in the header.
- **Plane · Home / Gusto · All caught up** — the empty state as an illustration and one line. Kept: All clear as it is.
- Our own Inbox (#176) and Insights / Listening bands (#172–#174): the band anatomy (cells divided by grey-20 rules,
  named number, sub-line, sparkline) and the featured card with the object beside the reason.

## Desk (`#/`)
- **Band** (one card): the moment's scene (mountains and lake, `BriefScene`) + « Good morning, Sam. » · **Need you**
  N + « K urgent » in red · **Handled alone** N + « since 18:40 yesterday » · two **facts** as small linked cells, each
  with its agent's avatar, a named number, its change and a sparkline: Iris's **Reach · 7 days**, Echo's **Share of
  voice** (the numbers the Insights and Listening bands lead with). Under a rule, the **six agents**: avatar in colour
  with its state dot, name, « N for you » or « On shift » / « Paused », each a link to its module; the line of what it
  did is its tooltip; the night in detail (Shift board, latest actions with Undo) stays behind « What they did ».
  No sentence, nothing that repeats the list.
- **Set apart**: the most urgent single card (`byPriority`: urgent first, then the oldest; a batch is never featured),
  only when Needs you holds 2 or more. Large card, alert-edge border when urgent; left: slip, title, context, Why,
  decisions; right: the object (reply with the customer's message, post, metric, plan). Decisions on the spot, no keys
  (the keys belong to the pane). Same rule as Focus's first card.
- **Your day** (§14 queue): Filters unchanged. Needs you is grouped by gesture — **Your call** (decisions, missing
  info, alerts, autonomy, news) then **To approve** (approvals and batches) — short header + count, none when only one
  gesture is there. Row = card: type icon in the agent's colour (32), title on 2 lines, one meta line (agent avatar,
  « Urgent » / « Today » named in colour, the context's gist), the object in miniature on the right (post visual,
  author avatar with network, metric curve, clip frame). A batch row stacks its first 3 items + count.
- **Worth a look / Good news**: small cards led by the number (metric value + change + curve; a good news without a
  metric shows the figure its title leads with) or the post visual, then the title. Hover and selection: blue border.
- **Pane**: Detail unchanged (faithful previews, decisions, keys). A **batch** reads as a group of small cards (one
  bordered card per item, author avatar 32 or post visual), Send N selected unchanged.
- Done and All clear unchanged.
