# Object shape — Inbox

Matrix §15. The Inbox stays a queue (§14), but a queue of **social conversations**: who wrote, on which network, in
what mood, and what Nova would send back. It opens on a short overview and sets the most urgent conversation apart.

## References (Mobbin)
- **Intercom · Inbox** — rows lead with the person, then one line of their message; the thread is a messenger (bubbles,
  avatars, outbound on the right). Kept: person first, message as the row's body, bubbles in the panel.
- **Quo · Chats** — a row shows « Draft: … » under the customer's line, and the AI suggestion sits as a card above the
  composer. Kept: the agent's reply previewed on the row, set apart by colour; the draft as a reply card.
- **Hootsuite · Inbox 2.0** — avatar with the network badge, a type icon (mail, comment) before the message, « You are
  replying » as the row's status. Kept: type as icon + word, network as the avatar badge.
- **Front · Analytics overview / Plain · First response time** — new conversations and reply time as two named numbers;
  Plain sets « breached threads » apart beside the response time. Kept: volume and response time against the target in
  one band; what is past target set apart, not buried in a report.
- **Gorgias · Support performance** — « human response time after AI handoff » next to the AI agent's share. Kept: what
  the agent handled alone, said in its sentence.

## Inbox › Needs you (`#/inbox`)
- **Overview band** (Needs you, no conversation open): Nova's one sentence with her avatar (« I handled 35 of today’s 49
  alone; @raph_m needs you first ») · **Waiting on you** N + « K past target » · **Response time** « 79% on target »
  (first replies today within their type's target, counting what waits past it) + median first reply · **New today** N +
  a sparkline per hour + the top networks as icons with counts.
- **Set apart**: the conversation to answer first is a large card above the queue. Rule: escalations (Your call) before
  the rest, then the furthest past its target, then the longest wait. The card shows the customer's message in full
  (the trigger words marked), the reason in one line + « Why? », Nova's reply (In public / Then by DM) in her colour, and
  the decisions: Send holding reply · Edit · Take over · Ask Claire.
- **Groups by gesture**, short headers with a count, no hint: **Your call** (escalations, you're replying) · **To check**
  (a passage Nova doubts) · **Ready to send** with its group primary « Send all N ». Proof: the « send Nova's ready
  replies » scenario becomes one visible click under one header, and the 5-second question (how many, which is urgent) is
  read from the headers and the card, not from scanning 13 identical rows.
- **Row** (conversation, not ticket): avatar 40 with the network badge; name · type (icon + word) · mood (coloured icon
  + word); the customer's line; Nova's reply on a peony wash with her small avatar (the doubted passage marked for To
  check, « Holding reply » for Your call); the wait named, red past target; one visible decision (Send / Send as is /
  Send holding reply), the rest (Edit, Take over, Ask Claire, Hide) in a menu.

## Other states (With the team, To review, Handled by Nova, Hidden, All, Views)
Same row anatomy: who · type · mood, the message, what Nova sent (Handled by Nova) on her wash, status pill only when it
differs, wait, labels, assignee, time. Sorted by date, the list is cut by day (Today · Yesterday · date), so « a
conversation from yesterday » is found by scrolling to its day. Filters, Views menu, selection and bulk unchanged.

## Conversation panel
- **Header**: avatar, name, type · network · time, and the contact in one compact line (customer since, orders, lifetime
  value, tags). The Contact tab keeps the orders.
- **Thread**: a messenger — their bubbles left, ours right (Nova's in her wash, a teammate's neutral), avatar beside,
  name and mono time above, notes full width on the caution wash.
- **Nova's draft**: a reply card in her colour; « In public » and « Then by DM » as two outgoing bubbles; product found,
  attachments, decisions unchanged.
