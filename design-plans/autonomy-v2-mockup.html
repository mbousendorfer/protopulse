What changes

  • · One line per action: icon, name, level. 52 px a row instead of ~120.
  • · The level is the app’s segmented control (Seg), the same as the presets, so it reads as buttons. The chosen step takes Nova’s colour.
  • · Actions grouped by risk (Low risk, Medium risk, Always you): the risk text leaves every row, and the presets make sense at a glance.
  • · Detail on demand: the chevron opens the example, what each level does (current one tinted) and the track record.
  • · After a change, one line under the row says what happens now (no message count).
  • · The “This week, about 303 messages” card becomes one line under the preset sentence.
  • · Presets and the Undo bar stay as they are.
