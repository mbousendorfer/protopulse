Protopulse UX audit

A heuristic review of every screen (Nielsen's 10 heuristics, Krug's "Don't make me think"), done in the live app at 1440×900 and 375×812 and in the code. Each issue is rated 1 (cosmetic) to 4 (blocks the task). 25 November 2026.

What held the score down

Three patterns caused most of the damage. Undo buttons that marked a line "Undone" while the reply stayed sent. Badges that gave three different numbers for the same work (Desk 37 in the menu, 25 on the page; Inbox 14, 13 and 10). Actions with real consequences on a single click: a €150 ad boost, the Autonomous preset, deleting a guardrail. A keyboard shortcut could also send a reply by accident: on the Desk, G then A opened Agents and approved the selected card.

    Undo didn't undo

    Undo on a Desk decision, a single Inbox send, the plan approval, the sales email and the campaign launch only crossed the line out. The card stayed in Done and the action stood.

    Undo on a Desk decision brings the card back and reverses what it changed (autonomy move, Playbook rule). A single Inbox send now gets the same 10-second Undo bar as Send all. Actions that can't be reversed (an email, a launched campaign) no longer offer Undo.

    Details

    Ask Iris has no visible send button. Alert rules delete without undo. The Inbox undo bar has no countdown. Monday's post is titled "Sunday reset". Report cards repeat their type in the title. On a phone, the Inbox filters push the list below the first screen.

To reach 10 / 10

Close the four "Still open" items that change patterns (toasts, Desk first screen, one autonomy vocabulary, one home for conversations), then test Focus and the Inbox with three or four people doing a real morning: the remaining risk is in how fast a first-time user trusts Send, not in the screens themselves.

How it was done

Every screen walked in the live prototype at desktop and phone widths, then three parallel code reviews (Desk and shell; Inbox, Publishing and Insights; Listening, Strategy, Agents, Playbook, Settings and Onboarding). Each finding was checked against the code before being fixed. Typecheck, 28 tests and the build pass; the fixes are live on GitHub Pages.
